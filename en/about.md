This blog collects writing about life and reading, alongside travel
and memories. Some pieces begin with an everyday moment;
others find their way back through a book.

Desire and choice, time and memory, and how we live with ourselves recur
throughout. The writing keeps a record of how these questions have looked
at different points in life.

Start with the [recent posts]({{ '/en/#posts' | relative_url }}),
or browse the [archive]({{ '/en/archive.html' | relative_url }}).
[Reading]({{ '/en/now/' | relative_url }}) collects reading by year.
